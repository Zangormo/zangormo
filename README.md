### 🃏 Featured project: Poker Tracker

Bank-keeping app for home poker cash games: tracks buy-ins, rebuys and cash-outs, reconciles final chip counts against money in, and settles the night as a short list of "A pays B" payments.

Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · MVVM · Hilt · Room · Coroutines / Flow
